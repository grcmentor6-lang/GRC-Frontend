1\. Purpose

Ensures that every Group organisation knows the legal, regulatory and contractual requirements that apply to it, tests that it meets them, and supports external audits and certifications with complete, reliable evidence.

2\. Scope and applicability

All legal, regulatory and contractual obligations of Group organisations, the control library that meets them, compliance testing, and every external audit, assessment or certification of a Group organisation.

This policy applies to every BKB organisation. Organisation addenda (BKB-ADD-01 … 42) record local legal requirements that are stricter than, or additional to, this policy. Where law and this policy conflict, the stricter requirement applies.

3\. Policy statements

3.1 Policy statements

• Each organisation keeps an obligations register: one row per obligation, with its source, owner, deadline and evidence of compliance.

• Regulatory change is monitored every month; the impact of a change is assessed within 30 days of its publication, and the obligations register updated (Section 6.1).

• Compliance Managers maintain the control library and run the annual compliance testing plan (GRC Charter Section 13).

• External audits and certifications are scheduled on the Group calendar and coordinated by the organisation's Compliance Manager (Section 6.2); auditors receive evidence only through the evidence request list and the evidence repository.

• Evidence given to an auditor meets the evidence-quality guideline (Section 7.1).

• Regulatory correspondence is signed off by Group General Counsel.

6\. Procedures

6.1 Procedure: Regulatory change management

| Step | Action | Responsible | Output / SLA |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Log each regulatory change found by monitoring | Policy & Governance Analyst | Change log entry — within 5 working days of publication |
| 2 | Assess which obligations, policies and controls the change affects, office by office (Section 7.2) | Compliance Manager | Impact assessment — within 30 days of the change's publication |
| 3 | Update the obligations register and raise actions for affected policies and controls | Compliance Manager | Register update and actions — before the change takes effect |
| 4 | Report changes with a material impact to the Org CRCO | Compliance Manager | Change report — monthly |

6.2 Procedure: External audit coordination

| Step | Action | Responsible | Output / SLA |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Agree the scope, the controls in scope and the audit period with the auditor | Compliance Manager | Audit plan |
| 2 | Build the evidence request list (PBC list): one request per control in scope, each written as the table below sets out | Compliance Manager | PBC list |
| 3 | Send each request to its owner | Compliance Manager | Requests sent |
| 4 | Provide the evidence requested, as an unchanged system export | Request owner | Evidence — by the request's due date |
| 5 | Check each file against Section 7.1 and index it to its request; return any file that fails | Compliance Manager | Evidence index |
| 6 | Support fieldwork: walkthroughs, interviews and the auditor's follow-up requests | Compliance Manager | Request log |
| 7 | Agree a corrective action for each finding and track it to closure | Control owners | Corrective-action plan |

Each request of the PBC list states:

| Field | How it is set |
| :-: | :-: |
| Evidence requested | The record the control produces each time it operates, exported from the system it runs in: the record that shows the control operated during the audit period. A report about a different activity, or a statement that the control works, is not evidence of it. |
| Population / query | Every occurrence of the control, from the system it runs in. |
| Period | The audit period agreed with the auditor. |
| Sample | The minimum sample for the control's frequency (rule R15, Ref-Sampling). |
| Owner | The role Responsible (R) for the control's process in the RACI of the GRC Charter (Section 16): the role that operates the control holds its records. The Accountable role (A) signs off the process but does not hold its files. |
| Due | Rule R16. |
| Acceptance criteria | Rule R17's sentence, word for word, on every request. |

Coverage gaps: once every file is checked and indexed (step 5), a control with fewer than two accepted files has a coverage gap (rule R18). The Compliance Manager sends the request's owner a follow-up request at once, in the words of rule R18. The missing files are due by the last working day (Monday to Friday) before fieldwork starts (rule R16): evidence that is not in when fieldwork starts is "no evidence" in front of the auditor.

7\. Guidelines

7.1 Guideline: Evidence quality

Dated: the date and time, or the system clock, are visible (D1), and fall inside the audit period (D4).

Attributable: the user or system that produced it is visible (D2); an approval shows who signed it and when (D8).

Complete: it covers the whole period (D3) and shows the query or filter that produced it (D5).

Unaltered: an unchanged system export, never annotated, edited or re-typed; notes go in a separate file (D6).

In scope, legible and safe: from the in-scope system, office and entity (D7); every key field legible (D10); no unmasked personal data or secrets (D9).

Every evidence request states these as its acceptance criteria, in the words of rule R17.

7.2 Guideline: Applicability of a regulatory change

A change the organisation addendum does not list yet is applied to each office in turn. It applies to an office when all three tests are met; record the result and the reason for every office.

| Test | The question | Where it is read |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 Reach | Does the law reach the office? A national law reaches an office in its country. A law that regulates a market, as many EU laws do, also reaches an office that offers its service to people in that market, wherever the office is. | The office's row of the addendum, Section 2; the source's own scope line |
| 2 Activity | Does the office run the kind of service or process the law regulates? | What the office runs (the addendum, Section 9, or the office's input file) |
| 3 Threshold | Is the organisation above any size or other threshold the law sets? A size threshold is measured on the enterprise as the law defines it, never on one office alone. Record the threshold in the brief either way. | The source; the organisation's headcount and turnover |

8\. Compliance, exceptions and enforcement

Compliance is measured through the KPIs below, control testing, internal audit and the GRC Operational Task Libraries.

Exceptions require a documented risk assessment, compensating controls, a named owner, an expiry date (maximum 12 months) and approval per the delegated authority in the GRC Charter Section 6.1.

Violations may result in disciplinary action in accordance with local employment law, contract termination for third parties, and reporting to authorities where legally required.

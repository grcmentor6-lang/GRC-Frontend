1\. Purpose

Sets the direction and principles for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information across the BKB Group, in a way that fits the purpose and the business of each Group organisation, and establishes the information security management system (ISMS) that puts them into effect.

2\. Scope and applicability

All information, systems, people, premises and suppliers of Group organisations, and every office listed in the GRC Charter, Appendix A.

This policy applies to every BKB organisation. Organisation addenda (BKB-ADD-01 … 42) record local legal requirements that are stricter than, or additional to, this policy. Where law and this policy conflict, the stricter requirement applies.

3\. Policy statements

3.1 Policy statements

• Each organisation operates an ISMS that conforms to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, with its scope recorded in its organisation addendum.

• Information security objectives are set each year at Group and organisation level, are measurable, and are tracked under Section 6.2.

• The Group commits to satisfy the legal, regulatory, contractual and information security requirements that apply to it.

• Risks to information are assessed and treated under BKB-POL-002 and BKB-ERM-001.

• Controls are selected from ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A and recorded in a Statement of Applicability for each organisation.

• Information security roles and responsibilities are assigned as the GRC Charter sets them.

3.2 Management framework

The Group commits to continual improvement of the ISMS: nonconformities are corrected, and the management review (Section 6.1) decides on improvements.

Internal ISMS audits cover every clause and applicable control at the coverage in Section 4.

This policy, the ISMS scope and the Statement of Applicability are kept as documented information under document control (Section 11).

4\. Criticality-band requirements

Minimum requirements scale with organisation criticality as defined in Organization\_Criticality\_Segregation (13 HIGH, 21 MEDIUM, 8 LOW organisations).

| Requirement | HIGH (GRC 501 orgs) | MEDIUM (GRC 301 orgs) | LOW (GRC 101 orgs) |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Management review frequency | Semi-annual | Annual | Annual |
| Internal ISMS audit coverage | 100% annually | 100% over 24 months | 100% over 24 months |
| External certification / attestation | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory where listed; otherwise recommended |

6\. Procedures

6.1 Procedure: ISMS management review

| Step | Action | Responsible | Output / SLA |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Collect the inputs of ISO/IEC 27001 clause 9.3: audit results, risk status, objectives, incidents, nonconformities | Compliance Manager | Review pack |
| 2 | Hold the review, chaired by top management | CISO | Meeting |
| 3 | Record decisions on improvements, resources and risk acceptance | CISO | Minutes with decisions |
| 4 | Track each decision to closure | Compliance Manager | Action log |

6.2 Procedure: Setting and tracking security objectives

| Step | Action | Responsible | Output / SLA |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Propose objectives from the risk assessment and last year's results | CISO | Draft objectives |
| 2 | Approve the objectives with targets, owners and dates | Org CEO | Approved objectives |
| 3 | Measure progress each quarter | Compliance Manager | Objectives dashboard |
| 4 | Report the results to the management review (Section 6.1) | CISO | Review input |

7\. Guidelines

7.1 Guideline: Tailoring the ISMS to the organisation

Start from the organisation addendum: its offices, laws and criticality band.

Keep the ISMS scope to what the organisation controls; record the interfaces with suppliers and the Group.

Reuse Group controls and evidence before writing local ones.

8\. Compliance, exceptions and enforcement

Compliance is measured through the KPIs below, control testing, internal audit and the GRC Operational Task Libraries.

Exceptions require a documented risk assessment, compensating controls, a named owner, an expiry date (maximum 12 months) and approval per the delegated authority in the GRC Charter Section 6.1.

Violations may result in disciplinary action in accordance with local employment law, contract termination for third parties, and reporting to authorities where legally required.

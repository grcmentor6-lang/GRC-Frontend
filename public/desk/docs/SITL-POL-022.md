1\. Purpose

Ensures that artificial intelligence is used in the Group only where its risks have been assessed, and that no confidential data reaches an AI tool the Group does not control.

2\. Scope and applicability

All use of AI tools and AI-enabled services by Group workforce, contractors and mentees, and all AI features the Group builds or buys.

This policy applies to every SITL organisation. Organisation addenda (SITL-ADD-01 … 42) record local legal requirements that are stricter than, or additional to, this policy. Where law and this policy conflict, the stricter requirement applies.

3\. Policy statements

3.1 Policy statements

Each AI use case is assessed for security, privacy and accuracy risk and approved before use.

Confidential (Tier 3) and Restricted (Tier 4) data is never entered into an AI tool the Group has not approved, whatever the account type or settings of that tool.

Removing names from a text does not make it safe to enter in an unapproved AI tool.

AI output is checked by a person before it is relied on.

6\. Procedures

6.1 Procedure: AI use-case approval

| Step | Action | Responsible | Output / SLA |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Submit the use case with the data it would use | Business owner | Request — before first use |
| 2 | Assess security, privacy and accuracy risk | Security Architect | Assessment — within 10 working days of the request |
| 3 | Approve, approve with conditions or refuse | Policy owner | Decision notice — within 5 working days of the assessment |

7\. Guidelines

7.1 Guideline: Using generative AI safely

In the GRC 101 programme no AI tool is used for task work. All help is pre-written on the platform, and work data never leaves the platform.

Elsewhere in the Group, where an approved AI tool is used: enter no Tier 3 or Tier 4 data, and no text of a client's, however it has been edited. Verify every fact and every citation an AI tool gives before it goes into a record, and cite the official source, not the tool.

8\. Compliance, exceptions and enforcement

Compliance is measured through the KPIs below, the register of approved use cases and internal audit.

Exceptions require a documented risk assessment, a named owner and an expiry date (maximum 12 months), approved per the delegated authority in the GRC Charter Section 6.1.

Violations may result in disciplinary action in accordance with local employment law, contract termination for third parties, and, for a mentee, a conduct breach under SITL-POL-024 Section 8.

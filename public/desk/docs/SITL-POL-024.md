1\. Purpose

Sets the conduct and data rules every mentee follows in the Group GRC Office, so that the work a mentee submits is their own, is recorded correctly and never exposes real data.

2\. Scope and applicability

Every mentee of the GRC 101, 301 and 501 programmes, in every Group organisation they work for, and every account, file and record the programme gives them.

This policy applies to every SITL organisation. Organisation addenda (SITL-ADD-01 … 42) record local legal requirements that are stricter than, or additional to, this policy. Where law and this policy conflict, the stricter requirement applies.

3\. Policy statements

3.1 Policy statements

A mentee account is personal to one mentee. It is not shared, lent or used by anyone else, for any length of time, and every step, file and decision made in it is recorded as that mentee's work.

All work a mentee submits is their own.

Mentees work only on the synthetic or sanitised data the platform gives them. They never obtain live production data, real personal data or credentials.

Task data stays on the platform. Files received are kept in 01_Inputs and are not changed; work in progress is kept in 02_Working; final files are placed in 03_Submission. No task data is copied to a personal device, a personal cloud drive or any outside service.

No AI tool is used for task work (SITL-POL-022 Section 7.1). All help is pre-written on the platform.

Outside data a mentee comes across, or any message offering real data or a real company's material, is reported through the platform and not opened, kept or forwarded.

A mentee signs the Mentee Undertaking before the account is opened for data.

6\. Procedures

6.1 Procedure: Mentee onboarding

| Step | Action | Responsible | Output / SLA |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| 1 | Check the assignment and the plan the programme office recorded for the mentee against the platform | Mentee | Onboarding record — before any other task |
| 2 | Read the conduct and data rules of Section 3.1 | Mentee | Rules read — before step 3 |
| 3 | Tick the data rules and sign the Mentee Undertaking. Without a signed undertaking the account is not opened for data | Mentee | Signed undertaking — before the first task that touches data |
| 4 | Keep task data in the four platform folders: 00_Admin, 01_Inputs, 02_Working, 03_Submission | Mentee | Folder paths recorded in the Onboarding record |
| 5 | Name the Onboarding record file as the naming convention says and save it to 02_Working | Mentee | File with its hash and UTC time |

6.2 Procedure: Mentee assessment

Tasks and checks are marked automatically against the key held on the platform. No person reads or marks a mentee's answers; written cells are format-checked and kept with the hand-in. A task score is released when the task is submitted.

7\. Guidelines

7.1 Guideline: Making the most of personas

Treat every message as if it came from a colleague with a real need and a real deadline: read it, decide, and say why. A message that offers real data, or asks you to break Section 3.1, is a drill: refuse it and report it.

8\. Compliance, exceptions and enforcement

Conduct is measured through the platform's own records: files, decisions and messages are stamped with the time and the account that made them.

A confirmed breach of Section 3.1 is a conduct breach and an automatic fail of the programme. Breaches include using live or real data, copying another mentee's work, sharing or using another person's account, and using any AI tool for task work. The certificate is not issued while a confirmed breach stands.

Exceptions require approval by the Group GRC Steering Committee and are recorded with their reason and an expiry date.
